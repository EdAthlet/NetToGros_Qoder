About these rates (2026)

Fixed personal credits match Revenue’s 2026 rates. Rent (max €1,000 / €2,000), Sea-going Naval (€1,500), Guide Dog & Assistance Dog tax credits (€165 each), and Mortgage Interest Tax Credit (max €625 — dedicated MITC guidance, not the €1,250 general-chart figure) are included for PAYE training.

Expense-based reliefs (health, tuition, flat-rate expenses, remote working, employing a carer, etc.) are not full checklist items — put residual or RPN-only amounts in Other annual tax credits from RPN.

Use the result with the Take Home Pay Calculator (manual tax credits) or the PAYE Lab for period-by-period application.
