Lesson 6 — Confirm and save the payroll run

✓ Completed

Aim

This lesson trains you to commit a calculated preview with Confirm & Save so the run is stored in History.

Steps

  1. In the top menu click Free Payroll Practice.
  2. On Your Companies click Load RPN practice sandbox (Recommended first).
  3. Click Confirm.
  4. Click the company name Cloud Sandbox Ltd.
  5. Click the RPN tab.
  6. Click Retrieve RPN.
  7. Click the Run Payroll tab.
  8. Click Calculate Preview.
  9. Click Confirm & Save.
  10. Click the History tab.

Done when

History lists the saved payroll run for Cloud Sandbox Ltd. This is practice data in this browser, not a live Revenue filing.
