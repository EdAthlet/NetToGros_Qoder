Lesson 4 — When Retrieve RPN fails

Stay on Free Payroll Practice in the training sandbox, where Retrieve RPN uses a fake Revenue practice connection, not live ROS.

  1. Load the RPN practice sandbox and open Daniel McCarthy, an employee whose PPSN ends in 0.
  2. Open the RPN tab and choose Retrieve RPN.
  3. Retrieve RPN should fail with a fake Revenue practice error.
  4. Read the message.
  5. PPSN ending 0 is a designed practice failure. Do not invent a live ROS connection.
