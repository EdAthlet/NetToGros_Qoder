Lesson 4 — When Retrieve RPN fails

✓ Completed

Aim

This lesson trains you to read the designed Retrieve RPN failure for a PPSN ending in 0.

Steps

  1. In the top menu click Free Payroll Practice.
  2. On Your Companies click Load RPN practice sandbox (Recommended first).
  3. Click Confirm.
  4. Click the company name Cloud Sandbox Ltd.
  5. Click the RPN tab.
  6. Click Retrieve RPN.
  7. Click Daniel McCarthy's row (PPSN 7567890IJ).

Done when

Daniel McCarthy shows Error: Invalid or unknown PPSN. That is a fake Revenue practice error, not live ROS.
