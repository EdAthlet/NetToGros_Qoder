Lesson 5 — LPT on the payslip

✓ Completed

Aim

This lesson trains you to check Sofia OBrien's Local Property Tax of €45.00 on the payslip.

Steps

  1. In the top menu click Free Payroll Practice.
  2. On Your Companies click Load RPN practice sandbox (Recommended first).
  3. Click Confirm.
  4. Click the company name Cloud Sandbox Ltd.
  5. Click the RPN tab.
  6. Click Retrieve RPN.
  7. Click the Run Payroll tab.
  8. Click Calculate Preview.
  9. Click Sofia OBrien's line in the calculated Preview table (PPSN 7234567CD).

Done when

The payslip shows LPT €45.00 for Sofia OBrien.
