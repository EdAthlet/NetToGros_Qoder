Lesson 5 — LPT on the payslip

Stay on Free Payroll Practice with the RPN practice sandbox. The payslip uses the fake Revenue practice connection, not live ROS.

  1. Load the RPN practice sandbox, then choose Retrieve RPN.
  2. Open Sofia OBrien, whose PPSN ends in 7.
  3. Run Calculate Preview and click her line.
  4. Confirm Local Property Tax €45.00 on that period. PPSN ending 7 / Sofia / LPT €45 is the check.
