Lesson 3 — Tax credits into Take Home Pay

Open Tax Credits and tick the credits that apply to the practice case. The page builds an annual total from those ticks. Choose Use in Take Home Pay. Take Home Pay opens with Manual tax configuration, and Annual Tax Credits is filled with that total.

Open Tax Credits · Open Take Home Pay
