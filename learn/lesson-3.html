Lesson 3 — Tax credits into Take Home Pay

✓ Completed

Aim

This lesson trains you to send a Tax Credits total into Annual Tax Credits on Take Home Pay.

Steps

  1. In the top menu click Tax Credits.
  2. Click Common: single employee.
  3. Click Use in Take Home Pay.

Done when

Take Home Pay shows Manual Input, and Annual Tax Credits is filled with 4000.00.
