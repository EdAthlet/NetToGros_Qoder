Lesson 1 — Load the RPN sandbox

✓ Completed

Aim

This lesson trains you to load the RPN practice sandbox so the practice employees are listed.

Steps

  1. In the top menu click Free Payroll Practice.
  2. On Your Companies click Load RPN practice sandbox (Recommended first).
  3. Click Confirm.
  4. Click the company name Cloud Sandbox Ltd.

Done when

The Employees list shows the sandbox people, including Daniel McCarthy (PPSN 7567890IJ) and Sofia OBrien (PPSN 7234567CD).

Lesson 2 — Retrieve an RPN and run a preview

✓ Completed

Aim

This lesson trains you to retrieve an RPN and open a payslip from Calculate Preview.

Steps

  1. In the top menu click Free Payroll Practice.
  2. On Your Companies click Load RPN practice sandbox (Recommended first).
  3. Click Confirm.
  4. Click the company name Cloud Sandbox Ltd.
  5. Click the RPN tab.
  6. Click Retrieve RPN.
  7. Click the Run Payroll tab.
  8. Click Calculate Preview.
  9. Click an employee's line in the calculated Preview table.

Done when

The payslip breakdown opens for that employee.

✓ Completed

Aim

This lesson trains you to send a Tax Credits total into Annual Tax Credits on Take Home Pay.
