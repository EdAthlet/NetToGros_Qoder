IPASS-like cumulative method (training card) click to expand click to collapse
Matches Irish Payroll Association style Cumulative Tax Deduction Card. E = Week × (SRCOP÷52); D accumulates taxable pay; F = max(0, D−E); G = min(D,E)×20%; H = F×40%; I = G+H; J = Week × (TC÷52); K = max(0, I−J); L/M = rise/fall in K vs previous week; N/O ≈ PRSI on gross. Edit B Gross / C Taxable to recalculate the card.
