Sample employee setup

Method (same idea as Free Payroll local mode): period COP = annual COP ÷ periods in year (week‑1 / month‑1 slice; unused COP does not roll). TC remained till year end at period start is the unused annual tax credit. If Start period > 1, period 1 is shown, then … for skipped periods (assumed even use of flat period TC), then your rows — opening TC = annual − (start−1) × (annual ÷ periods in year). Going forward, period tax credit = remaining TC ÷ periods still left. Taxable@20% = min(taxable pay, period COP); @40% = excess. Gross PAYE = 20% + 40% bands; Applied TC = min(period TC, gross PAYE); Net tax = gross − applied. Yellow cells are drivers / overrides; green dashed cells are calculated. Double‑click an orange override to restore auto.
Practice 1: the answer key is generated from the setup (same rules as the worksheet). Click an empty cell to open the formula builder above the table. Drag the correct values into the slots (the operation sign is fixed). When the result looks right, paste it into the field. Use Check at the end of each row to mark correct / incorrect cells. Taxable pay is arbitrary for training: periods sample under/over COP and smaller vs larger tax against the period credit — not real payroll figures. If Start period > 1, opening TC remained till year end is a 3-part quest: annual TC − (prior period count × flat period TC) — multiply the skipped periods (including period 1) by the assumed periodic credit; do not pick the pre-multiplied total from a single oval.
