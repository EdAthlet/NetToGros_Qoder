Single PSPS Contribution Calculator

Simple estimator for the Single Public Service Pension Scheme member contribution per pay period. This version covers SPSPS entrants from 1 January 2013. More schemes and use cases will be added later.

Results

Contribution breakdown

Indicative calculator only — not financial or legal advice. Rates follow the SPSPS contribution structure (3% pensionable remuneration + 3.5% net pensionable remuneration). Net pensionable remuneration deducts twice the annual State Pension Contributory reference (€15,617.47) pro-rated to the pay period.
